# Lifecycle Modeling Language (LML) Certification Overview 
Based on the LML v2.0 specification [https://www.lifecyclemodeling.org/hubfs/LML%20Specification%20v2.0-1.pdf?hsLang=en], the exam is time-boxed to 120 minutes and has seventy (70) multiple-choice questions. Examinees may request training or self-study.
